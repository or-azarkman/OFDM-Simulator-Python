## 3. Data Consistency — OK

**BER (AWGN, 5000 symbols):**
- BER decreases with increasing SNR (e.g. ~0.16 at 0 dB → ~0 at 16–20 dB for QPSK). Expected.

**Comparison table (summary/ber/):**
- AWGN has lowest BER at each SNR; Multipath (no eq) has highest; ZF and MMSE lie in between. Expected.
- 16-QAM has higher BER than QPSK at same SNR. Expected.

**EVM (summary/evm/):**
- EVM (%) decreases with SNR. Expected.
- MMSE has lower EVM than ZF at low SNR (e.g. 0 dB: MMSE ~73%, ZF ~109%). Expected.

**Pilot-based comparisons:**
- Known channel performs better than or equal to pilot-based estimation. Expected.
- 16-QAM shows larger gap between known and pilot-based at high SNR than QPSK. Expected (channel estimation errors affect higher-order modulation more).
